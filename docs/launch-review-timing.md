# Launch review timing

Checked against the public contract and source evidence available on 11 October 2026. The current ROBIN builder keeps a maximum 86,400-second plan interval. Its default starts 60 seconds before preparation, so its deadline is 23 hours and 59 minutes after preparation. The wallet handoff requires the issued Atomic order's `validAfter` and `deadline` to equal that packed plan interval, then independently requires a current approval and fresh successful transaction simulation. No deadline is extended locally.

There is a conflict in the platform's published timing evidence:

- The [current manifest](https://api.programmable.market/v4/chains/4663/custom-launch-contract/manifest.json), with manifest digest `sha256:3cd25308629f932d6a75e4a7694e6bcb67e4471fd2c1782d3b11ba5a7e3f8b61`, declares Atomic `orderValidity: "exact_plan_valid_after_and_deadline"`.
- The [verified Atomic executor source](https://sourcify.dev/server/v2/contract/4663/0xe21827818Fea1F14d59929CAE36a93c0B44e0aE1?fields=all) rejects any signed order with `deadline - validAfter > 86400`. The live capability binds this executor and runtime hash `0x9c97ca89d17ec48ba9603920f6a27899e83ac0c4514f9acb7a37f4903d374fad`.
- The [platform wallet verifier at public revision 1ecc3bad](https://github.com/programmablehq/PROGRAMMABLE/blob/1ecc3bad8b3a147ea00ae86aa0c47990def1f313/lib/custom-launch/atomic-wallet-review-v2.ts) accepts a narrower order interval contained within the plan interval. Its [Atomic codec](https://github.com/programmablehq/PROGRAMMABLE/blob/1ecc3bad8b3a147ea00ae86aa0c47990def1f313/lib/custom-launch/atomic-plan-codec-v2.ts) separately limits that order to 86,400 seconds. However, its [current tests and fixture](https://github.com/programmablehq/PROGRAMMABLE/blob/1ecc3bad8b3a147ea00ae86aa0c47990def1f313/tests/fixtures/atomic-launch-v2.ts) copy the plan timestamps directly into the order; they do not demonstrate issuance of a fresh 24-hour order inside a 48-hour plan.
- The [launch guide](https://api.programmable.market/v4/chains/4663/custom-launch-contract/guide.md) describes fresh unsigned transaction deadlines after approval and advises allowing review time plus the full 24-hour launch window. ROBIN's initializer contains no embedded timestamp. The owned review timing assessment reported no decoded embedded deadlines, with three undecoded project actions; that assessment does not establish issuer behavior.

The request packer's structural rule only requires the plan deadline to follow its start. Passing that rule with a 48-hour envelope would not prove that the protected issuer supports narrowing it. No public issuer implementation or successful issued package establishing that behavior was found. Therefore this release retains the strict 24-hour binding. A longer plan envelope requires confirmation of the released issuer contract, followed by inspection and simulation of its actual returned order, before changing the local guard. Expired or replaced packages require the documented new-package/replan flow; they are never silently extended or signed.
